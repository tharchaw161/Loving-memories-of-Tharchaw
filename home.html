Our Story

Around 2017, I found Tharchaw alone on the street. As someone who has always loved cats, I couldn’t leave him there, so I brought him home and gave him a place to stay. That was the beginning of nearly nine years together. He became part of my everyday life, my companion, and my family. Sadly, in 2025, he crossed the Rainbow Bridge, leaving behind a home full of memories and a place in my heart that will always belong to him.

You came into my life unexpectedly, but you became family. Thank you for growing up beside me, waiting while I studied, sleeping near me, and filling my home with memories. Until we meet again. 🐾
